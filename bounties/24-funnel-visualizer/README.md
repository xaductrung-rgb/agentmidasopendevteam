# Bounty #24: Funnel Visualizer ($250)

Conversion Funnel Builder with Stage Reordering, Step-by-Step Drop-Off Analysis, and Bottleneck Detection.

## Features
- **Full Funnel Stage CRUD & Reordering**: Create, rename, update visitor counts, reorder (move up/down), and delete funnel stages.
- **Step Conversion & Drop-Off Analytics**: Calculates stage-to-stage conversion rate, cumulative conversion from top of funnel, drop-off count, and highlights the largest bottleneck step.
- **Persistent Storage & Rollback**: Saves funnel stages to `localStorage` and invokes async `onChange` with rollback on error.
